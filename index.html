    Rider Safety & Protocols

    Your security on Nepal's high-altitude and winding terrain is our absolute priority.

    Certified Local Pilots

    Every companion rider undergoes rigorous background screening, holding extensive documented experience on Himalayan gravel tracks, monsoon mud paths, and high mountain passes.

    Top-Tier Gear Standards

    DOT/ECE-approved full-face helmets, reinforced abrasion jackets, knee guards, and communication headsets are supplied fresh for every individual journey.

    Emergency Route Protocols

    Our rides maintain satellite-enabled check-ins for remote passes like Mustang and Manang, backed by localized medical evacuation logistics partners.

    Two ways to pay

    Pick Bank Transfer or eSewa when you check out — you'll scan the correct QR and enter your transaction reference right there.

    🏦 Bank Transfer

    Pay directly from any mobile banking app to our Laxmi Sunrise savings account. Your bank's app-generated QR or transfer reference works as your proof of payment.

    📱 eSewa

    Scan & pay instantly using the eSewa app. Your eSewa transaction ID is all our team needs to verify the payment.
