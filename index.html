Rider Safety & Protocols

Your security on Nepal's high-altitude and winding terrain is our top priority.

Certified Local Pilots

Every rider holds extensive documented experience on Himalayan gravel tracks, monsoon mud paths, and high mountain passes.

Top-Tier Gear Standards

DOT/ECE-approved full-face helmets, reinforced abrasion jackets, knee guards, and communication headsets supplied fresh.
