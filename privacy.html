Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Feel It Nepal

Who we are

Feel It (“we”) operates guided motorbike and pillion tours in Nepal. Contact: the email and WhatsApp listed on our website.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone (when you register or book)
  • Booking details: tour, dates, travelers, payment method, transaction reference, optional payment proof image
  • Messages you send through the site
  • Basic analytics (pages visited) only if you accept cookies

How we use it

  • To confirm bookings, assign riders, and deliver your ride briefing
  • To verify payments and prevent fraud
  • To reply to your messages and improve our service

Sharing

We share only what is needed with your assigned local rider (name, phone, meeting details) and payment processors you choose (e.g. bank / eSewa). We do not sell your data.

Storage & security

Data is stored in Supabase (cloud database) and, where needed, on secure staff devices. Payment screenshots are used only for verification. No site is perfectly secure; use strong passwords and never share OTPs.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep you logged in and remember theme preference. Analytics cookies run only after you accept the cookie banner.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your booking/account data by messaging us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services are aimed at adults. Minors should book only with a parent or guardian.
